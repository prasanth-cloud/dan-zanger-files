# Minervini SEPA/VCP Qualification Checklist

Use this to tag each chart entry from Trade Like a Stock Market Wizard (Vol 1 & 2).

## Trend Template (all must be true)

- [ ] Price above 150-day MA
- [ ] Price above 200-day MA
- [ ] 150-day MA above 200-day MA
- [ ] 200-day MA trending up for at least 1 month
- [ ] 50-day MA above 150-day and 200-day MA
- [ ] Price above 50-day MA
- [ ] Price at least 25% above 52-week low
- [ ] Price within 25% of 52-week high

## VCP (Volatility Contraction Pattern)

- [ ] Base count (T1, T2, T3...): ___
- [ ] Each contraction tighter than prior (Y/N)
- [ ] Depth of corrections decreasing
- [ ] Volume dry-up in later contractions
- [ ] Pivot point identified (price): ___

## Breakout Confirmation

- [ ] Volume surge on breakout day (vs 50-day avg): ___x
- [ ] Price closes in upper range of day's bar
- [ ] No excessive gap (>20% above pivot)
- [ ] Follow-through within 1-3 days
